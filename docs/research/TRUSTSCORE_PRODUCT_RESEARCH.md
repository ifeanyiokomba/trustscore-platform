# TRUSTSCORE PRODUCT RESEARCH — Position, Wedge, Moat

**Date:** 2026-09-17 · **Inputs:** NINAuth official research, competitor research, Termii design research, repository audit.

---

## 1. The problem (concrete, not abstract)

Nigeria's digital economy runs on strangers: marketplace sellers, Twitter-trade counterparties, artisans via WhatsApp, rental applicants, gig workers. NINAuth proves *who someone is* — but every counterparty still asks "can I deal with this person?" Trust is verified fresh each time, by hand, with no receipts, no due process, and no portability. Fraud thrives in that gap; good strangers pay the tax.

## 2. The one-line thesis

> **NINAuth proves the identity. TrustScore builds the portable trust layer around that identity.**

## 3. Why now / why us

- NINAuth gives a government-backed, consent-driven, biometrically-bound identity rail — the hardest primitive now exists as infrastructure.
- The layer above it is empty: KYC vendors sell lookups (COGS), nobody sells *portable, person-owned, revocable trust with due process*.
- Network effects compound on the verifier side: every Safety Check, receipt, and resolved dispute enriches the graph for the next checker.

## 4. The five verbs (product packaging)

```
VERIFY  — turn your NINAuth identity into a Trust Passport (freshness-honest)
CHECK   — check before you deal (consent-gated, receipted, never "safe")
SHARE   — scoped expiring links + QR Trust Cards (you control what/whom/how long)
PROTECT — flags with evidence, review, appeals; anti-gaming by design
RESOLVE — disputes with human review, receipts, and corrected scores
```

## 6. What TrustScore is NOT (standing list)

Generic KYC vendor · raw-NIN lookup site · public blacklist · credit bureau · bank-data aggregator · universal good/bad-person score.

## 7. Monetization hypothesis (unpriced until pilot evidence)

- Consumer: premium passport features (verified credentials, richer receipts)
- B2B: Trust Decision API + verification APIs + developer platform — priced per *decision*, not per lookup, after provider/infra/fraud/support costs and willingness-to-pay are measured (Batch 15 metrics).

## 8. Honest risks

1. NINAuth partner onboarding (intermediary-gated, ~5–7 business days/step, IP allow-listing) — the LIVE gate is real; sandbox-first is mandated.
2. Cold-start network effects — Safety Check value scales with verified-member density; pilot market selection matters.
3. Trust-score liability — mitigated by design (explainability, appeals, NEW-neutrality, "assessment not decision" language).
4. Trademark hygiene — "TrustScore" vs Prove's "Trust Score®": keep distinct styling/copy; legal pass recommended.

## 9. Pilot readiness metrics (what we measure before scaling)

Identity-verification completion rate · NINAuth success rate · verification latency · Safety Check usage · Passport sharing rate · decision outcomes · appeals (volume, overturn rate) · false positives · fraud attempts · B2B API usage · operational cost.
