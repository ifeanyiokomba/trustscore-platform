   486→// ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   487→// Stage 6 — Safety Check (directive §2/§46 "Check Before You Deal").
   488→// A signed-in VERIFIER runs a sanitized assessment on a subject via:
   489→//   HANDLE        — the subject's @handle (requires their standing safety
   490→//                   consent; anti-enumeration: "no such handle" and "checks
   491→//                   disabled" are byte-identical responses)
   492→//   PHONE         — consent-gated peppered-hash match against the subject's
   493→//                   PHONE identifier (the raw phone is hashed and discarded;
   494→//                   it is NEVER stored, logged or echoed)
   495→//   TRUST_LINK/QR — an active Stage 5 share token / QR Trust Card payload
   496→//                   (token scopes govern; the view is counted and receipted
   497→//                   with the verifier's NAME — a named check)
   498→// Every completed check writes a SafetyCheck row (the snapshot the verifier
   499→// saw), a receipt + notification for the subject, and an audit event.
   500→// The assessment NEVER says "this person is safe" (directive §50).
   501→// ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   502→
   503→model SafetyCheck {
   504→  id           String    @id @default(cuid())
   505→  verifierId   String
   506→  subjectId    String
