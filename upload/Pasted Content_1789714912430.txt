# 4. CORE STRATEGIC ARCHITECTURE CHANGE

TrustScore must evolve from:

**“NIN verification product with additional features”**

into:

**“Identity and Trust Verification Orchestration Platform, initially anchored by NINAuth.”**

NINAuth remains the foundational high-assurance government-backed identity source.

But the system must be capable of combining multiple evidence sources over time.


# 38. EMAIL QUOTA MANAGEMENT

Google Apps Script currently documents a consumer-account email-recipient quota of 100/day, with quotas resetting 24 hours after the first request and subject to change.

Therefore do not hard-code assumptions such as:

```text

```

The router must instead track:

```text
configured limit
observed remaining quota
last quota observation
safe threshold
provider health
critical reserve
```

Similarly, provider limits such as those from Resend must be configurable rather than permanently embedded in business logic.
