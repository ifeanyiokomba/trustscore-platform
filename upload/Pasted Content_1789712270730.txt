# 27. EMAIL PROVIDER 2 — GOOGLE APPS SCRIPT

Integrate:

**Google Apps Script**

The intended sender account is:

`support@okomba.com`

This is NOT assumed to be a Google Workspace mailbox.

Treat the Google account as a constrained provider.

Google's current Apps Script documentation lists consumer-account email-recipient quota at approximately:

**100 recipients/day**

while Workspace limits are higher; quotas can change and reset based on the quota rules rather than necessarily resetting at midnight.

Therefore:

DO NOT hard-code:

```text
100 forever
```

Instead:

```text
provider configured limit
provider observed usage
safety threshold
critical reserve
remaining capacity
```

The system should discover, monitor and adapt.

# 54. IMPLEMENTATION QUALITY STANDARD

Do not optimize for:

**“It works.”**

Optimize for:

**“It is correct, secure, explainable, maintainable, resilient, tested, observable and professionally designed.”**

Every implementation decision must answer:

1. Why is this required?
2. What problem does it solve?
3. What existing workflow could it affect?
4. What are the failure modes?
5. What happens when the provider is unavailable?
6. What happens at quota exhaustion?
7. What happens under attack?
8. What happens when evidence is insufficient?
9. How is the behavior tested?
10. How will this scale later?
